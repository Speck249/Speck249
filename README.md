# Hi there, I'm Rediet :wave:  

:dancer: A Product Owner at the intersection of product intent and engineering reality. <br>

:key: I own the technical implementation of product decisions, turning user needs and business constraints into working systems with social and commercial value. <br>

:telescope: My goal is to lead the delivery of reliable, evaluable intelligent systems that are genuinely useful - not just feature-complete.

---

### What I Bring as a Product Owner

:white_check_mark: **Technical Implementation Ownership** <br/>
Accountable for translating product decisions into concrete engineering work and ensuring the implementation meets the intent.
  
:white_check_mark: **Backlog & Delivery Clarity** <br/>
Refine technical backlog items, define clear acceptance criteria, surface risks early, and keep engineering focused on value.

:white_check_mark: **Cross-functional Translation** <br/>
Bridge product, design, and engineering conversations so constraints and opportunities are visible to everyone.

:white_check_mark: **Evidence-oriented Thinking** <br/>
Prefer measurable outcomes and structured evaluation over gut-feel prioritization.

:white_check_mark: **Systems Perspective** <br/>
Comfortable thinking about architecture trade-offs, technical debt, data readiness, long-term maintainability, and documentation that survives hand-offs.

---

### How I Work

I believe the best Product Owners:
- Make engineering constraints visible early
- Write requirements that are implementable, not just aspirational
- Treat AI features with extra rigor around evaluation and failure modes
- Leave the codebase and the team better than they found them

---
